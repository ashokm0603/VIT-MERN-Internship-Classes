Browser Storages: 
------------
    1. localstorage 
            ->localstorage is the permanent Storage 
            ->data will not delete until users deleted manually 
            ->in localstorage data stored in the form of KEY and VALUE

            Methods : 
             1. localStorage.clear()
             2. localStorage.removeItem('name')
             3. localStorage.key(0)
             4. localStorage.getItem(keyName)
             5. localStorage.setItem(key , value)
       
    2. session Storage 
            ->sessionStorage  is the temporary Storage 
            ->data will  delete automatically when tab closed
            ->in sessionStorage data stored in the form of KEY and VALUE
            Methods : 
             1. sessionStorage.clear()
             2. sessionStorage.removeItem('name')
             3. sessionStorage.key(0)
             4. sessionStorage.getItem(keyName)
             5. sessionStorage.setItem(key , value)

    3. cookies
        ->cookies are used to website related data / tokens 
        ->Here data stored directly key-value
